# V5 design — competing-risks discrete-time hazard (adversarially reviewed)

*Produced by a 9-agent research workflow: 4 researchers (survival methods, small-n
statistics, behavioral economics, prior art) -> synthesis -> 3-auditor refutation
panel (leakage / statistics / product) -> revision. This is the revised spec; the
implementation in src/analyzer/hazard_data.py, eval/hazard.py and
scripts/train_hazard_model.py follows it. Sections below are verbatim.*

## model family

TWO pooled discrete-time hazard models (Singer–Willett person-period penalized logistic regression, fixed L2 lam=1.0, sklearn LogisticRegression with the pure-numpy fit_logistic in eval/model.py as fallback), extending src/analyzer/hazard_data.py + eval/hazard.py: (A) TEAM-EXIT cause-specific hazard h_A — risk set = coins whose gated team has not yet sold AND that have not yet died; (B) COLLAPSE hazard — risk set = coins not yet collapsed, with 'team_exited' and log1p(time-since-exit) as time-varying covariates. REVISED per panel: the death-without-exit hazard h_D needed for competing-risks composition is model B evaluated at team_exited=0 on the not-yet-exited risk set — no third model, but this imposes shared covariate slopes across the 0→2 (~40 events) and 1→2 (~160 events) transitions; stated as an explicit bias/variance assumption, checked by exactly ONE budgeted team_exited×covariate interaction race, never claimed as asymptotic equivalence. Composition is the competing-risks forward-recursion CIF (Putter–Fiocco–Geskus 2007 Stat Med 26:2389; Austin & Fine 2017 Stat Med 36:4391), NEVER 1−prod(1−h) (the 1-KM error: ~17% of coins die without team exit, concentrated exactly in the quoted 5–20min horizons). Calibration via ONE shared Platt map applied identically to all horizons (independent per-horizon sigmoids can re-cross — the exact defect the 6 binary heads were attacked for); Platt-not-isotonic stays project law. Non-shipping companions: ridge Cox C-index canary per fold; shallow monotone-constrained GBM on the same person-period rows as a DEFERRED challenger raced only at ~1000 classic dense trajectories (one-line TRAINERS swap).

## target structure

Predicted object: per-interval cause-specific hazards on a grid pre-registered ONCE before any fitting as the sorted union of DEFAULT_EDGES and EARLY_CHECK_SECONDS (0,30,60,90,120,180,210,240,300,390,420,480,600,720,900,1200,1800,2400,2700,3600 → 19 intervals; baseline shape is carried by smooth tv_log_t, not per-interval dummies, so the finer grid costs zero parameters and makes every live checkpoint an exact interval boundary — no mid-interval landmark mismatch). The open 60min+ interval is DELETED: under expand_coin's own censoring rule (tape_span_s < b → break) an unbounded interval can never receive an event-free row, so its risk set is outcome-selected and the hazard degenerates to 1.0 — the grid truncates at 3600s with ADMINISTRATIVE CENSORING T_admin=3600s (event-free rows require span ≥ interval end AND a liveness witness); the ~10-15% cure fraction is expressed as CIF(3600)<1, and the prior 'hazard→0 absorbs the cure' claim is withdrawn. EVENTS: (A) first sell by a GATED team member (existing expand_coin semantics). (B) sustained collapse as a DELAYED STOPPING TIME, not the non-local MIN_TRADES_AT_PEAK mirror (trajectory.py's n_at_moon counts prints over the whole tape; the naive mirror backdates collapse across genuine recoveries for ~39% of collapsers, deleting alive-time rows based on FUTURE prints): candidate = first print below 0.5× the ROBUST first-price anchor (median of first k≥3 priced prints — pts[0][1] as a lone denominator re-admits the single-bad-print artifact); confirmed iff ≥3 sub-0.5× prints occur within a bounded window W of the candidate (W sensitivity-swept); if unconfirmed and a sustained recovery (≥3 prints above 0.5×) intervenes, the candidate is VOIDED and the search restarts after the recovery. Decidable with ≤W lookahead; live risk-set membership therefore has a pending-confirmation state ≤W, and shadow deployment logs the train/serve risk-set divergence it causes. (B') liquidity-death requires a GLOBAL-LIVENESS WITNESS: declared only when (i) no trades for X s with robust last price (median of last k≥3 prints) < 0.7× anchor, (ii) other coins' tapes provably printed during the same wall-clock silence window (ingest alive), and (iii) the tape does not end at a collection-window boundary or known outage window (Mac-mini-sleep c974535, 2026-07-08 provider outage, Supabase drops) — otherwise CENSOR at last print. On this tape, silence is DOMINANTLY ingest truncation, not market death: 149 of ~156 thin-tape coins come from one 3-day burst (2026-07-22/24) with near-uniform truncation spans; that burst is QUARANTINED from label-of-record regardless of X, so the thin-tape recovery mechanism realistically adds only ~7 historical coins plus forward accrual — the earlier '~156 recovered rows' claim is withdrawn. Event-timestamp convention (at last print vs last print + X) is itself a sensitivity axis. Silence-as-death applies to model A's risk set too: death removes a coin from A's risk set as a MODELED competing hazard, never silent right-censoring. OUTPUTS: (1) PRE-WARNING at graduation = CIF_A(h) = Σ_i h_A,i·Π_{j<i}(1−h_A,j−h_D,j) for h in {5,10,20,40,60} min, scored in T+0 MODE — TV covariates fixed at train-set per-interval medians (primary; a statics+tv_log_t-only refit is the robustness diagnostic) — because composing over REALIZED TV covariates (current horizon_prob) is a sequential forecast, not a T+0 prediction, and would grade the graduation alert on information it cannot have; (2) LIVE ESCALATING ALARM = conditional next-interval hazards re-scored at the ACTUAL EARLY_CHECK_SECONDS cadence (120,210,300,390,480,600,720,900,1200,2400 — the tape is already fetched there; the old 5/10/20/40 schedule post-dates the median 2.4-min exit for most coins and is detection, not escalation), landmark-conditional mode, covariates computed strictly before the checkpoint's grid edge; (3) SEVERITY after a team exit = model B's collapse hazard in the team_exited=1 stratum, surfaced as calibrated hazard BANDS with re-send only on upward band crossing and a pre-registered max update count per coin (the incumbent fires once; undisciplined escalation converts the best tier into noise). Every logged probability persists WHICH scoring mode produced it so track-record claims cite the matching backtest. Monotonicity across horizons holds for the raw CIF recursion and is preserved by the single shared Platt map. No moon/2×/10× event exists anywhere in the event set.

## training protocol

Population: classic pump.fun only, positively platform-gated, manufactured excluded (both already in load_person_periods' SQL); 2026-07-22/24 thin-tape burst quarantined from label-of-record. Splits: expanding walk-forward by graduated_at with COIN-GROUPED folds (eval/hazard.py coin_folds), first fold trains on earliest 40%. Regularization: FIXED L2 lam=1.0, {0.3,1,3} grid as diagnostic only (Riley 2021: tuned shrinkage is noise below ~20 EPV). Imputation: train-stats median impute; missing indicators ONLY for statics with a known missingness mechanism — this must be IMPLEMENTED (current _prep hstacks an indicator for EVERY column, doubling 14 features to 28 estimated columns and halving true EPV); TV covariates get no indicators. ENTITY-HISTORY STATICS (funder rug rate, graph_rug_hits, launches_24h, any reputation-derived feature) are reconstructed AS-OF each coin's graduation from event-timestamped source tables — prior coin graduated AND outcome resolved strictly before this coin's graduated_at — never read from current-state incremental tables (funder_reputation backfill injects future outcomes; the project's exact burn class, invisible to the single-feature ROC canary at the ~0.75-0.85 level such leaks produce); EB hyperparameters (alpha,beta) refit PER TRAINING FOLD on train-period-resolved outcomes only. Calibration: one shared Platt map fit on composed CIF horizon probabilities via an inner coin-level time-split of train, refit only when the inner split provides n≥50 coins (fold-1's inner split leaves ~23-28 — below gate, inherit the prior map). Drift: weekly recalibration is TWO parameters — a single shared intercept shift plus one tilt on tv_log_t (van Houwelingen 2000 Stat Med 19:3401; Steyerberg 2004 intercept-first hierarchy) — the original 14 per-interval offsets on trailing 50-100 coins (~2-7 events per interval, zero late) is degenerate and withdrawn; optionally offsets shrunk toward full-history values. LABEL MATURITY for every weekly-updated quantity (Platt map, recalibration params, alert threshold, EB hyperparameters): a coin enters a trailing refit window only if graduated_at + T_admin < refit time. Escalation to full slope refit only when pooled-interval AUC over ≥100 trailing MATURE coins degrades with a cluster-bootstrap CI excluding the historical value — never on per-week point estimates. Optional sign-constrained fit raced vs unconstrained on identical folds (a fought constraint is a logged finding). SHIP GATES (all on fold-AVERAGED metrics with coin-cluster-bootstrap CIs — pooled-concatenated AUC across refit models under an 80→94% base-rate drift is biased and asymmetrically favors the refitting model over the fixed rules function; Forman & Scholz 2010, Airola et al. 2011): (1) T+0-mode fold-averaged CIF_A(10min) AUC beats verdict_rules_v2 replay by ≥0.05 AND matches/beats the classic-only-refit team_exit10 binary head at the SAME information set within CI; (2) calibration slope in [0.8,1.2] on a PRE-REGISTERED window of ≥100 pooled recent mature coins, decision rule = cluster-bootstrap CI excludes gross miscalibration (slope <0.5 or >2), never a 28-coin point estimate; (3) pre-warn precision at the train-derived threshold on the trailing window ≥ the number that will be publicly claimed (the hardcoded-0.90 incident is the cautionary precedent); (4) the escalating alarm does NOT degrade the live reactive alarm's precision/median-%-saved on the same coins — the incumbent for deliverable 2 is the reactive detector (docs/TRACK_RECORD.md: 547 fired, 100% of graded correct, median 88.2% saved), NOT verdict_rules_v2 (ROC 0.58, trivially low bar) — with hazard value quantified as lead time gained before the first team sell; (5) severity bands appear in alert text only after model B calibration passes in the team_exited=1 stratum under its own separate n-gate; (6) uv run python -m eval.audit full pass exits 0; (7) shadow-first beside the incumbents with hard-SKIP rules staying IN FRONT (near-deterministic facts outrank probabilities), per eval/MODEL_BASELINE.md Phase 6. Per-fold test sets are 27-28 coins — below the project's own n≥30 gate — so per-fold numbers are hypothesis-level by the project's own rule; only fold-averaged aggregates are quotable.

## evaluation protocol

Metrics (coin level, fold-AVERAGED primary with coin-cluster-bootstrap CIs; pooled-concatenated reported only as a secondary diagnostic): interval AUC; horizon AUC at {5,10,20,40,60} min for team-exit CIF and collapse, each n-gated at ≥30 at-risk coins, GRADED SEPARATELY PER SCORING MODE (T+0 vs landmark-conditional — never grade the graduation tier on realized-TV compositions); time-dependent Brier; per-interval reliability; and the product number — operating-point alert precision/recall at a stated alert rate. Pre-warnings are graded from graduated_at (not send time) with delivery latency logged in prewarn_alerts — 38% of exits land inside 30s, before delivery completes. The track-record generator MECHANICALLY enforces quoting only 5-20min horizons until late horizons pass their n-gates, grades tape-silent coins as collapse-confirmed ONLY via the liveness-witnessed liquidity-death rule (fixing the 430-of-547-pending selection bias — the gradable-subset precision stands on silence-is-censoring, the same artifact fixed in training), and reports ungradable counts explicitly. Uncertainty: coin-cluster bootstrap for all coin-level derived metrics; the '~8x overstatement' claim is DELETED (rows/coins = 6.5, so 8× is arithmetically impossible even at ICC=1; the person-period likelihood is a genuine likelihood with conditionally independent Bernoulli terms — Allison 1982; Singer & Willett 1993 — so hazard parameters need no cluster correction under correct specification; the bootstrap is kept for misspecification robustness and derived metrics). Never report row counts as sample size; effective n = coins bounded by events. Baselines: (1) for the graduation tier — classic-only refit team_exit10/survive60/fastrug binary heads at the same information set, plus verdict_rules_v2 replay; (2) for the escalation tier — the LIVE REACTIVE ALARM's precision/median-saved (incumbent), with hazard value = lead time; (3) ridge Cox C-index canary; (4) for model B severity — a price-proximity-only baseline (tv_price_vs_first + tv_drawdown + tv_log_t alone): collapse hazard near the 0.5× boundary is partially detection, and severity claims must show structural/sell-structure features add rank skill beyond 'price is already approaching the threshold'. All mixed-era numbers (ROC 0.91-0.94, AND the 80%-vs-64% team-leads-collapse discrepancy between the spec and trajectory.py's own docstring) go through classic-only re-measurement before appearing in any shipped doc or gate. LEAK AUDITS in eval/audit.py (non-zero exit blocks deploy): (a) TV-covariate source-trade timestamp < interval t_start, mechanical over the whole expansion; (a2) NEW — AS-OF AUDIT FOR STATICS: recompute a sample of entity-history features from time-filtered source tables and assert equality with training values (audit (a) covers TV only; statics previously had no mechanical check and the ROC canary cannot see entity-history leaks); (b) landmark scoring drops coins whose event occurred ≤ the landmark; (c) EXTENDED event-time audit: sample collapse stamps and assert the ≥3 confirming prints are LOCAL to the stamped timestamp (within W) — the previous wording passed the backdating pathology; (d) informative-censoring sensitivity: sweep X∈{180,300,600}s AND the event-timestamp convention AND with/without thin-tape coins AND liveness-witness on/off; a large hazard-curve shift means the censoring model, not the market, drives results; (e) funder-blocked folds beside time folds; (f) terminal untouched time slice with a NUMERIC look budget: max 3 looks, then the slice retires and a new one is frozen. INCREMENT-TEST POWER RULE: at ~139 pooled test coins the detectable AUC increment between nested correlated models at 80% power is ~0.04-0.08, so every increment is logged with its cluster-bootstrap CI and classified 'falsified' vs 'not detectable at n=X, re-test at n≥Y' — an underpowered null must never enter NEGATIVE_RESULTS as a permanent ban. Run the Riley 2020 (BMJ 368:m441, pmsampsize) minimum-sample criteria once for the actual parameter count and event fraction and quote the result; EPV≥10 is the weakest available criterion (van Smeden 2016 BMC Med Res Methodol 16:163).

## behavioral grounding

Shumway (2001, J. Business) grounds the core swap: static fixed-horizon classifiers on duration data are biased and inconsistent; the multi-period logit is the consistent, more data-efficient estimator — why the 6-head suspension resolves into one model. Singer-Willett (1993, J Educ Stat 18:155) / Allison (1982, Sociological Methodology 13:61) ground the person-period mechanics AND the corrected uncertainty story: the pooled likelihood is a genuine likelihood, so cluster corrections belong to derived coin-level metrics and robustness, not to a fabricated variance multiplier. Putter-Fiocco-Geskus (2007, Stat Med 26:2389) and Austin-Fine (2017, Stat Med 36:4391) ground the competing-risks CIF composition replacing the 1-KM formula. Van Houwelingen (2000, Stat Med 19:3401) / Steyerberg (2004) ground the 2-parameter intercept-first recalibration hierarchy for base-rate drift — explicitly starting at 1-2 parameters, which is what refuted the 14-offset weekly refit. Riley 2020 (BMJ m441) and van Smeden 2016 ground the sample-size arithmetic that replaced bare EPV>=10. Forman-Scholz (2010) / Airola (2011) ground fold-averaged-over-pooled AUC under drift. Prospect-theory reference dependence (Kahneman-Tversky 1979; corroborated by docs/TEAM_INTENT.md: median revealed target 1.07x, 69% dump <1.2x) grounds the price-level-contingent hazard — the covariate is the price NOW, the event strictly future. Preemption/coordination theory (Abreu-Brunnermeier 2003; Morris-Shin global games; Diamond-Dybvig run logic) grounds exit overhang denominated in pool depth and the cohesion-x-size interaction — first-principles adaptation, no crypto precedent, flagged as such. Crawford-Sobel (1982) grounds why uncostly holding is uninformative — hence forgone-exit-value as the only informative hold measure. Kyle (1985) grounds tv_net_flow_recent (informed sellers camouflage in retail inflow). Efron-Morris (1975) grounds EB shrinkage below the hard n-gates — now with the leak-safe as-of construction, since a prior computed on the future is not a prior. The sign-constraint layer is the operational form of 'theory supplies direction, data supplies magnitude'. The delayed-stopping-time collapse definition is grounded in the optional-stopping requirement of survival analysis itself: an event time whose definition reads future prints is not measurable w.r.t. the filtration, argued from first principles against the codebase's own non-local MIN_TRADES_AT_PEAK precedent. The team-leads-collapse figure is quoted as 64-80% pending classic-only re-measurement (the repo disagrees with itself: trajectory.py says 64%, earlier docs 80%).

## rejected alternatives

- Resurrecting the 6 independent binary GBM heads (wait for n>=500 each): statistically dominated — six fragments of the same tiny sample, six drifting calibrations, crossable horizon probabilities, Shumway-style bias; the hazard model trains today.
- XGBoost/LightGBM AFT as primary: misspecifies the duration mixture (2-min exit mass + long tail + cure fraction), emits point log-times not calibrated per-interval probabilities, n far below where boosting pays.
- Random survival forests: small-n simulations favor penalized statistical models, calibration weak under heavy censoring, TV covariates awkward; challenger at n>=1000 only.
- Continuous-time Cox as champion: proportional hazards violated by construction (violently front-loaded hazard); kept only as C-index canary.
- Explicit 3-transition multi-state model: the 0->2 direct-collapse cell has ~40 events, below the significance comfort once split by fold; the team_exited-covariate fold uses all ~200 collapse events — now stated as a shared-slopes ASSUMPTION with one budgeted interaction check, not asymptotic equivalence.
- Stacked Fine-Gray subdistribution models: CIFs can sum past 1; cause-specific hazards with forward-recursion CIFs are the standard prediction recommendation and are free in discrete time.
- 1-KM horizon composition P(exit<=h)=1-prod(1-h_A,i): estimates a latent no-death-world probability, upward-biased by the early competing-death incidence exactly at the quoted horizons; replaced by the competing-risks CIF recursion (Putter 2007; Austin-Fine 2017).
- Open unbounded final interval for cure absorption: degenerate under the person-period censoring rule — no event-free row can ever enter, risk-set membership is outcome-selected; replaced by administrative censoring at T_admin=3600s.
- Naive sustained-collapse mirror of MIN_TRADES_AT_PEAK (non-local print counting): backdates collapse across genuine recoveries for ~39% of collapsers, deleting alive-time from the risk set using future prints; replaced by the delayed-stopping-time rule with confirmation locality.
- Silence-as-death without a global-liveness witness: ingest truncation is the DOMINANT thin-tape mechanism on this tape (149/156 in one 3-day burst); the unwitnessed rule fabricates era-clustered collapse events that poison a whole walk-forward fold, and the X sweep cannot detect it because every X misreads truncation identically.
- Backfilling entity-history features from current-state tables (funder_reputation SQL as originally specified): injects post-graduation outcomes into training features — the project's exact burn class, invisible to the existing ROC canary; replaced by as-of reconstruction plus a mechanical static as-of audit.
- Weekly per-interval baseline-offset refits (14 free params on trailing 50-100 coins): ~2-7 events per interval, zero late — degenerate MLE, arbitrary shrinkage artifacts, +/-0.4-0.6 logit noise inherited by the alert threshold; replaced by 2-parameter intercept+tv_log_t-tilt recalibration (van Houwelingen 2000).
- Independent per-horizon Platt maps: can re-cross the horizons, forfeiting the monotonicity argument used against the 6 heads; replaced by one shared map.
- Bayesian horseshoe/hierarchical partial pooling as PRIMARY: heavy dependency, slow iteration, benefit approximated by fixed-L2 + walk-forward + n-gates; offline research twin later.
- TabPFN v2 as default: no native censoring, no drift story, black box; at most a one-afternoon challenger under identical folds — a surprise win is treated as a leak until proven otherwise.
- Deep/sequence models: ~0.01 AUPRC over an MLP at n=41K in MemeTrans; at n=231-387 pure variance — structure, not capacity, is the bottleneck.
- Isotonic calibration, recency/decay weighting, SMOTE/rebalancing, random or K-fold splits, in-sample feature selection, penalty tuning at this n: each falsified by project measurement or small-n literature (van den Goorbergh 2022; Riley 2021).
- Any moon/pump/2x/10x head, long-side entry gating, or social/attention layer under the survival framing: five independent falsifications; a hazard costume does not repair a target with no signal (corrected ROC 0.517).
